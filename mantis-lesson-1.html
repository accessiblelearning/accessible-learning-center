Mantis Q40 Lesson 1

Orientation, Power, and Status

Mantis Q40 manualAll Mantis Q40 lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create an orientation guide naming major controls and their purposes.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Place the Mantis Q40 on a stable surface and locate the QWERTY keyboard.
  2. Identify the 40 braille cells, cursor routers, thumb keys, and ports by touch.
  3. Power on the device using the documented button.
  4. Read the startup status and battery information.
  5. Practice moving through the main menu without changing settings.
  6. Power off safely.

JAWS and keyboard tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Commands can vary by software version and device model; use the official manual linked from the course manual when a control differs.
