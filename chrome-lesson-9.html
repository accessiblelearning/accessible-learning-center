Chrome Lesson 9

Privacy, Safe Browsing, Pop-ups, and Notifications

Chrome manualAll Chrome lessonsMy progress

Goal

Recognize browser warnings and manage privacy choices safely.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open Chrome Settings and review Privacy and security.
  2. Review Safety Check without disabling protections.
  3. Review Safe Browsing and do not select No protection.
  4. Review Site settings for notifications, pop-ups, camera, microphone, and location.
  5. Remove or block only a clearly identified unwanted practice permission.
  6. Do not bypass a deceptive-site or dangerous-download warning.
  7. Use Ctrl+Shift+Delete only to review the Delete browsing data dialog, then cancel without clearing shared data.
  8. Explain that Incognito limits local history but does not make activity invisible to websites, employers, schools, or internet providers.
  9. Close Settings and confirm normal browsing still works.
  10. Write a privacy-and-security checklist in Chrome-Lesson-9.txt.

Screen-reader help: With JAWS, press Insert+T for the window title, Insert+Tab for focused-control information, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Chrome controls and labels can change after browser updates.
