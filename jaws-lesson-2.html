Screen Readers Lesson 2

Keyboard Help, Speech Control, and Basic Information

Screen Readers manualAll Screen Readers lessonsMy progress

Choose your screen reader

Complete this lesson with JAWS, Windows Narrator, or NVDA. Use one primary screen reader at a time. Follow the column for your selection whenever commands differ.

Goal

Use keyboard help and control speech without losing focus.

Shared practice steps

  1. Open the screen reader selected in Lesson 1.
  2. Use its keyboard-help command and press several harmless keys to hear their descriptions.
  3. Exit keyboard help before returning to work.
  4. Press Ctrl while speech is talking to stop speech.
  5. Use the current-focus command to identify the selected control.
  6. Use the window-title command to confirm the active application.
