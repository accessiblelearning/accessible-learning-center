Focus 40 Blue Lesson 8

Use Scratchpad, BRF Book Reader, and microSD Safely

Focus 40 Blue manualAll Focus lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create a local note and a storage-safety checklist; submit only your original note.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open the fifth-generation Focus Scratchpad from the Menu.
  2. Create a short note and save it with a descriptive TXT or BRF filename.
  3. Close and reopen the note to verify it was stored.
  4. Open an instructor-provided BRF in the Book Reader and practice panning and bookmarks.
  5. If microSD storage is used, verify the source and destination before copying a practice file.
  6. Use the documented safe removal process and never copy or submit protected books.

JAWS and braille tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Focus commands vary by device generation, JAWS settings, application, and connection method; use the official guide linked from the manual when a command differs.

Your assignment

Create a local note and a storage-safety checklist; submit only your original note.

Use fictional, public, instructor-provided, or redacted information only. Never submit passwords, pairing codes, serial numbers, protected books, or private workplace data.

Save your work with a clear filename beginning Focus-Lesson-8.

Before submitting

  • All requested steps are complete.
  • The file contains no credentials or sensitive information.
  • Commands are identified as Focus, JAWS, Windows, or application commands.
  • The filename identifies Focus 40 Blue Lesson 8.
