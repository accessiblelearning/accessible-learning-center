The focused practice page blocks the included practice commands from reaching the webpage whenever the blue practice area has focus. It cannot block operating-system or protected browser commands everywhere.

Commands such as Alt+Tab, Windows+E, Windows+D, Ctrl+Alt+Delete, Ctrl+W, and F5 are not included for physical practice. A running screen reader may also intercept its own commands before the page receives them.
