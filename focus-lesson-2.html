Connect by USB and Configure JAWS

Focus 40 Blue manualAll Focus lessonsMy progress

Goal

Write a USB connection and JAWS verification checklist.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Start JAWS before connecting the Focus.
  2. Connect the approved USB-C cable to the Focus and computer; USB supplies power and data.
  3. Wait for Windows and JAWS to recognize the display.
  4. If braille does not appear, open JAWS Options, Braille, and verify Focus is the active display without removing other needed configurations.
  5. Press Insert+J, then Insert+T, and confirm speech and braille identify the expected window.
  6. Open a short document and verify that panning buttons move through the displayed text.

JAWS and braille tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Focus commands vary by device generation, JAWS settings, application, and connection method; use the official guide linked from the manual when a command differs.
