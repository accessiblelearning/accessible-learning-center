PowerPoint Lesson 8

Color, Contrast, Fonts, and Accessible Design

PowerPoint manual All PowerPoint lessons My progress

Goal

Apply a professional design that remains readable with low vision and does not communicate by color alone.

Skills practiced

  • Use a consistent built-in theme.
  • Check text and background contrast.
  • Use readable font sizes.
  • Add text labels for color-coded information.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open PowerPoint-Lesson-7.pptx.
  2. Use Alt+Q to search for Themes and choose a simple built-in theme.
  3. Keep body text large enough to read from a distance. Use approximately 18 points or larger for body text and larger sizes for titles when the layout allows.
  4. Keep strong contrast between text and its background.
  5. Do not place text over a busy image unless a solid background makes it readable.
  6. If a status is shown in red, yellow, or green, also write words such as Needs Action, In Progress, or Complete.
  7. Avoid rapidly flashing content and excessive animations.
  8. Keep the same typeface and heading treatment across slides.
  9. Review each slide at increased zoom and with JAWS object navigation.
  10. Run Check Accessibility and fix contrast or color-use warnings when available.
  11. Save the presentation as PowerPoint-Lesson-8.pptx.

Keyboard help: Press Alt+Q to search PowerPoint commands when Ribbon KeyTips differ. For JAWS help, press Insert+Spacebar, then J, and search for the task.
