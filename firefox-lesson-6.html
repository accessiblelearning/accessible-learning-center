Make a page easier to see

What you will learn: Adjust page size without changing every Windows application.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press Ctrl+Plus to enlarge the page. Press Ctrl+Minus to reduce it.
  2. Press Ctrl+0 to return to the normal zoom level.
  3. For text-only zoom, open the View menu with the keyboard, find Zoom, and select Zoom Text Only. Compare the result on a familiar page.

What should happen: You can choose a readable size and return to the baseline.

If that does not happen: A very large setting may change the layout. Reduce it one step and check whether content becomes easier to follow.

Practice: Compare one heading, one paragraph, and one button at two sizes. Choose the size that helps you complete the task, not simply the largest one.

Source: Mozilla accessibility features.

Read an article in Reader View

Start here: A text-heavy article in Firefox on Windows. Reader View is not available for every page.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Press F9 to request Reader View. If your laptop assigns another action to its function keys, use the keyboard’s appropriate function-key mode or use Firefox’s View menu and its Reader View command.
  2. Read the simplified page heading and a paragraph. Tab to the Reader View controls to explore its appearance options.
  3. Adjust one text or appearance setting, then return to the article and compare the result. Choose a size that lets you track a full line comfortably.
  4. Use the Reader View exit control or return to the original page. Check that you are back at the website before trying its ordinary navigation.

Check your result

On a supported article, you get a simpler reading presentation and can return to the original.

If something goes wrong

If Reader View is unavailable, use ordinary page zoom and your screen reader’s reading navigation. Do not expect account forms, dashboards, or every PDF to convert into an article.

Try it yourself

Compare the same paragraph in the original page and Reader View. Explain which presentation makes it easier to follow.
