Windows 11 Lesson 7

Quick Settings, Notifications, Audio, and Connectivity

Windows 11 manualAll Windows 11 lessonsMy progress

Goal

Review common system status and adjust only safe, temporary controls.

Skills practiced

  • Open Quick Settings and Notification Center.
  • Check volume and audio output.
  • Review network and battery status.
  • Dismiss or preserve notifications appropriately.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Press Windows+A to open Quick Settings.
  2. Use Tab and Arrow keys to review Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane mode, volume, battery, and other available controls. Do not toggle connectivity on a workplace computer.
  3. Move to the volume slider and identify its current value. Use Arrow keys only if changing volume is appropriate.
  4. Review the selected audio output device. Do not change it if another person or assistive device depends on the current setting.
  5. Press Escape, then press Windows+N to open Notification Center and calendar.
  6. Review notifications with Tab and Arrow keys. Do not activate unknown links or clear work-related alerts.
  7. Dismiss only a harmless practice notification if one is available; otherwise leave notifications unchanged.
  8. Open sound output settings with Windows+Ctrl+V if supported and review available devices.
  9. Close the panel and confirm JAWS or other speech still uses the intended output.
  10. Create Windows-Lesson-7.txt documenting the panels, status information, and three changes that should require caution.

Screen-reader help: These lessons work with JAWS and other screen readers. With JAWS, press Insert+T to confirm the active window, Insert+Tab to hear the focused control, and Insert+1 to toggle Keyboard Help. Windows features and labels can vary slightly after updates.
