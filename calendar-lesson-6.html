Google Calendar Lesson 6

Notifications and Reminders

Google Calendar manualAll Google Calendar lessonsMy progress

Goal

Design a notification plan for three kinds of work events.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open a practice event and locate notifications.
  2. Compare email, desktop, and other available notification choices.
  3. Set a reasonable reminder interval for a fictional meeting.
  4. Avoid excessive reminders that create alert fatigue.
  5. Save and verify the notification summary.
  6. Review browser notification permissions separately.
