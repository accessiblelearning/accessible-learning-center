Screen Readers Lesson 4

Navigate Windows, Dialogs, and Controls

Screen Readers manualAll Screen Readers lessonsMy progress

Choose your screen reader

Complete this lesson with JAWS, Windows Narrator, or NVDA. Use one primary screen reader at a time. Follow the column for your selection whenever commands differ.

Goal

Move reliably through Windows applications and identify focus.

Shared practice steps

  1. Open the Start menu and search for Settings.
  2. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls.
  3. Use Arrow keys within lists, menus, and groups.
  4. Press Spacebar to toggle a checkbox and Enter to activate a button when appropriate.
  5. Press Alt+Tab to switch applications and use the screen-reader focus command.
  6. Press Esc to close a menu or cancel a practice dialog without changing important settings.
