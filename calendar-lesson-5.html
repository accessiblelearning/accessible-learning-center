Google Calendar Lesson 5

Invite Guests and Respond to Invitations

Google Calendar manualAll Google Calendar lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create a guest-permission and invitation-response guide.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Create a fictional event and locate Add guests.
  2. Do not enter or send to real addresses during practice.
  3. Review guest permissions and remove unnecessary privileges.
  4. Study Yes, No, and Maybe response controls on an invitation.
  5. Add a brief response note only when appropriate.
  6. Record etiquette for updates and cancellations.
