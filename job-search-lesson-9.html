Accessible Job Search Lesson 9

Interview and Accommodation Planning

Accessible Job Search manualAll Accessible Job Search lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create an interview and accommodation preparation checklist.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Prepare short examples using situation, task, action, and result.
  2. Practice joining an accessible virtual meeting early.
  3. Test audio, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader settings.
  4. Decide whether and when an accommodation request is useful.
  5. Describe the job-related need and effective accommodation, not unnecessary medical detail.
  6. Prepare questions about duties, team, and next steps.
