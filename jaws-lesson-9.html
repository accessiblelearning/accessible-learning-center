Screen Readers Lesson 9

Help, Settings, Updates, Braille, and JAWS AI

Screen Readers manualAll Screen Readers lessonsMy progress

Choose your screen reader

Complete this lesson with JAWS, Windows Narrator, or NVDA. Use one primary screen reader at a time. Follow the column for your selection whenever commands differ.

Goal

Find trustworthy help, make reversible settings changes, and understand product-specific advanced features.

Shared practice steps

  1. Open the selected screen reader’s settings or menu.
  2. Locate speech, verbosity, keyboard-layout, and braille options without changing unrelated settings.
  3. Change one instructor-approved reversible setting, test it, and restore the original.
  4. Open the official help or current command reference.
  5. Record the installed screen-reader version without including device serial numbers or license information.
  6. Review the JAWS-only AI note and do not assume the same feature exists in Narrator or NVDA.

Command note: JAWS key, Narrator key, and NVDA key each mean that screen reader’s modifier key. Commands can vary by keyboard layout, software version, application, and user settings. Confirm differences in the official references linked from the manual.
