PowerPoint Lesson 2

Slide Layouts, Titles, and Organizing a Presentation

PowerPoint manual All PowerPoint lessons My progress

Goal

Use built-in slide layouts and meaningful titles to create a clear presentation structure.

Skills practiced

  • Add slides with Ctrl+M.
  • Choose a built-in slide layout.
  • Give every slide a unique title.
  • Reorder slides from the thumbnails pane.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open PowerPoint-Lesson-1.pptx.
  2. Press Ctrl+M to add a third slide.
  3. Press Alt+Q, type Slide Layout, and choose Title and Content.
  4. Title slide 3 Training Schedule and add three fictional schedule items.
  5. Add slide 4 and title it Contact Information. Add a fictional department email and telephone extension.
  6. Press F6 until JAWS announces the thumbnails pane.
  7. Use Up Arrow and Down Arrow to review slide titles.
  8. Select the Contact Information slide. Press Ctrl+X, move to the position after the title slide, and press Ctrl+V to place it second.
  9. Review the new order by moving through the thumbnails pane.
  10. Confirm that every slide has a unique title.
  11. Save a copy as PowerPoint-Lesson-2.pptx.

Keyboard help: Press Alt+Q to search PowerPoint commands when Ribbon KeyTips differ. For JAWS help, press Insert+Spacebar, then J, and search for the task.
