Screen Readers Lesson 8

Read, Edit, and Review Workplace Documents

Screen Readers manualAll Screen Readers lessonsMy progress

Choose your screen reader

Complete this lesson with JAWS, Windows Narrator, or NVDA. Use one primary screen reader at a time. Follow the column for your selection whenever commands differ.

Goal

Use shared editing commands and screen-reader feedback in Word or another document editor.

Shared practice steps

  1. Open a practice document and press Ctrl+Home.
  2. Use Ctrl+F to locate a required phrase.
  3. Select text with Shift plus navigation keys.
  4. Use Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C, and Ctrl+V to move or copy text.
  5. Use Ctrl+Z to undo an intentional edit.
  6. Navigate true headings and lists with the chosen screen reader, then save a copy.
