Goal

Submit a full screen-reader-friendly cybersecurity response plan.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Review a fictional urgent payment request.
  2. Inspect sender, links, attachment name, and requested action with JAWS.
  3. List at least five warning signs or reassuring indicators.
  4. Choose safe verification and reporting actions.
  5. Explain password, MFA, update, and backup protections.
  6. Do not interact with any real suspicious message for the assignment.

JAWS and keyboard tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Commands can vary by software version and device model; use the official manual linked from the course manual when a control differs.
