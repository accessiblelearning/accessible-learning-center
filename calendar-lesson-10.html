Goal

Submit a complete accessible weekly scheduling plan.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Plan five fictional events for one workweek.
  2. Include one recurring event and one deadline.
  3. Use clear titles, realistic durations, and useful descriptions.
  4. Apply sensible notifications without inviting real people.
  5. Review the schedule in chronological order with JAWS.
  6. Export the plan as a TXT, DOCX, PDF, or BRF report.

JAWS and keyboard tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Commands can vary by software version and device model; use the official manual linked from the course manual when a control differs.
