Search for the correct book and edition

Start here: Bookshare in a browser. Have the title and author, and an ISBN if your assignment specifies one.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Tab to the site’s book-search field. Enter a distinctive title or author and activate Search. An ISBN can help identify a particular edition.
  2. Read the results heading and count. Move through the results using links or your screen reader’s page-reading commands, rather than assuming the first result is correct.
  3. Open a likely result and review its title, author, description, and available publication information. Compare those details with the assignment.
  4. If the edition is wrong, return to the results or perform a narrower search. Record the correct title before opening the reader.
  5. If you are exploring rather than seeking an assignment, use the site’s browsing categories or Explore options. Treat recommendations as choices, not as required lesson order.

Check your result

You can explain why the selected book matches your reading goal.

If something goes wrong

If there are no matches, check spelling and try fewer distinctive words. A book with the same short title may be by a different author. If required edition information is unclear, ask the instructor before starting page-specific work.

Try it yourself

Find one title by author and another by a distinctive title phrase. Verify each author before reading.
