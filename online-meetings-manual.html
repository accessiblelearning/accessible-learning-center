Online meeting skills

Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet

Join, participate, present, and leave meetings with clear confirmation.

Mute and unmute

Keyboard commands: Zoom on Windows: Alt+A. Teams on Windows: Ctrl+Shift+M. Google Meet: Ctrl+D.

  1. Confirm the correct meeting application is active.
  2. Press its microphone command once.
  3. Wait for the spoken or sound confirmation.

What you should hear: Muted or unmuted, or the accessible name and state of the microphone button.

If that does not happen: Locate the microphone button and read its state. Do not repeatedly toggle an uncertain control.

Control the camera

Keyboard commands: Zoom: Alt+V. Teams: Ctrl+Shift+O. Google Meet: Ctrl+E.

  1. Confirm the active meeting and camera policy.
  2. Press the command once.
  3. Listen for stopped or started video.

If that does not happen: Check the named camera control and device permissions. A host or organization may have disabled it.

Open meeting panels

Zoom commands: Alt+U opens Participants, Alt+H opens meeting chat, and Alt+Y raises or lowers your hand.

  1. Open one panel at a time.
  2. Confirm its heading or accessible name.
  3. Complete the task and return focus to meeting controls.

If that does not happen: Review the application's current keyboard-shortcut settings; commands vary by platform and customization.

Share safely

Keyboard command: Zoom commonly uses Alt+S to start or stop sharing when permitted.

  1. Close private messages, files, and notifications.
  2. Choose the exact window instead of the full screen when possible.
  3. Confirm the selected source before starting.
  4. Provide an accessible alternative to visual content.

If that does not happen: Cancel the chooser and verify the window name and permission before trying again.
