Basic how-tos

Join a meeting safely

Keyboard commands: Tab and Shift+Tab move through join controls. Space changes a focused checkbox. Enter activates Join.

  1. Open the invitation and verify the organizer, date, and meeting link.
  2. Open the link and confirm the meeting application or browser tab.
  3. Tab through microphone, camera, name, and audio choices.
  4. Join muted unless instructed otherwise.
  5. Activate Join and wait for the meeting title or waiting-room announcement.

You should hear: Your microphone and camera states, followed by the meeting or waiting room.

If that does not happen: Do not press Join repeatedly. Check for a browser permission dialog or an “open application” prompt.

Mute or unmute

Keyboard commands: Zoom: Alt+A. Teams: Ctrl+Shift+M. Google Meet: Ctrl+D.

  1. Confirm the correct meeting is active.
  2. Press the command once.
  3. Wait for a spoken or sound confirmation.
  4. When uncertain, Tab to the microphone button and read its state.

If that does not happen: Do not toggle repeatedly. A host or organization may prevent unmuting.

Start or stop the camera

Keyboard commands: Zoom: Alt+V. Teams: Ctrl+Shift+O. Google Meet: Ctrl+E.

  1. Confirm the meeting and whether video is appropriate.
  2. Press the correct command once.
  3. Listen for “start video” or “stop video,” which usually describes the action available next.

If that does not happen: Locate the camera button and check camera permissions or host restrictions.

Open chat and send a message

Keyboard command: Zoom: Alt+H opens or closes meeting chat. In Teams and Meet, locate Chat by button navigation because shortcuts and layouts change.

  1. Open Chat and confirm its heading.
  2. Move to the message field.
  3. Type a short message and review it.
  4. Press Enter to send only when the application indicates Enter sends.
  5. Return to meeting controls after confirming the message appeared.

If that does not happen: Check whether focus is in chat history, a recipient selector, or the message field.

Advanced how-tos

Raise and lower your hand

Keyboard command: Zoom: Alt+Y. In Teams and Meet, locate Reactions or Raise hand by its button name.

  1. Activate Raise hand once.
  2. Listen for confirmation.
  3. Wait to be recognized.
  4. Lower your hand after speaking.

If that does not happen: Find the button and read whether its current action is Raise hand or Lower hand.

Turn captions on

Keyboard approach: Navigate to Captions, Closed captions, or More actions, then activate the captions control.

  1. Open captions before important discussion begins.
  2. Select the spoken language if requested.
  3. Confirm that caption text is appearing.
  4. Remember that automatic captions may misidentify names or technical words.

If that does not happen: Ask the host whether captions are enabled and request an accessible transcript or notes.

Share a window safely

Keyboard command: Zoom: Alt+S starts or stops sharing when permitted. For Teams and Meet, activate the labeled Share control.

  1. Close private files, messages, and notifications.
  2. Open only the content you intend to share.
  3. Start sharing and choose a specific window rather than the entire screen.
  4. Read the selected window name before confirming.
  5. Describe visual content aloud and provide the source file or accessible alternative.
  6. Stop sharing and confirm that sharing ended.

If that does not happen: Cancel the chooser. Never select a source whose name you cannot verify.

Leave without ending the meeting for everyone

Keyboard approach: Find Leave, then read the confirmation choices.

  1. Save anything needed from chat according to the meeting policy.
  2. Activate Leave.
  3. If you are the host, choose Leave meeting unless you truly intend to end it for everyone.
  4. Confirm that focus returns to the desktop or previous application.

If that does not happen: Read the dialog carefully; do not choose End meeting for all by accident.

Quick command list

  • Zoom Alt+A / Teams Ctrl+Shift+M / Meet Ctrl+D: microphone
  • Zoom Alt+V / Teams Ctrl+Shift+O / Meet Ctrl+E: camera
  • Zoom Alt+U: Participants
  • Zoom Alt+H: Chat
  • Zoom Alt+Y: Raise or lower hand
  • Zoom Alt+S: Start or stop sharing
  • Tab / Shift+Tab: next or previous control

Version note: Commands shown are for Windows. Web and desktop versions can differ.
