Microsoft Word Lesson 4

Formatting Text and Paragraphs

Goal

Practice bold, italics, underline, alignment, and basic paragraph formatting.

Instructions

  1. Create a blank Word document.
  2. Type the title: Accessible Technology Skills
  3. Press Enter twice.
  4. Type: Technology can increase independence at work, school, and home.
  5. Press Enter twice.
  6. Type: Learning keyboard commands can make common tasks faster.
  7. Make the title bold.
  8. Center the title using Ctrl+E.
  9. Make the words increase independence italic.
  10. Underline the words keyboard commands.
  11. Make sure both body paragraphs are left aligned using Ctrl+L.
  12. Save the document as Word-Lesson-4.docx.

Your Assignment

Submit Word-Lesson-4.docx.

Before Submitting

  • The title should be bold and centered.
  • The requested phrase should be italic.
  • The requested phrase should be underlined.
  • The body paragraphs should be left aligned.
