Focus 40 Blue Lesson 9

Braille Settings, Study Mode, Cleaning, and Updates

Focus 40 Blue manualAll Focus lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create a safe settings, cleaning, and firmware checklist.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open JAWS Braille settings and identify the active translation table without changing it.
  2. Review VariBraille or firmness settings on a supported fifth-generation Focus and change only an instructor-approved reversible value.
  3. Enable Braille Study Mode in JAWS and press a cursor router to hear the character in that cell.
  4. Restore the original settings after practice.
  5. Clean the display only with the power disconnected and the method in Freedom Scientific’s official cleaning instructions.
  6. Check the exact model and generation before any firmware update; never install fifth-generation firmware on an older Focus.

JAWS and braille tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Focus commands vary by device generation, JAWS settings, application, and connection method; use the official guide linked from the manual when a command differs.
