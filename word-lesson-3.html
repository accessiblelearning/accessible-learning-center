Microsoft Word Lesson 3

Find, Replace, Save, Close, and Reopen

Goal

Learn how to find text, replace text, save a document, close it, and reopen it.

Instructions

  1. Create a new Word document.
  2. Type three short paragraphs containing the word computer at least four times.
  3. Press Ctrl+F.
  4. Search for computer.
  5. Move through the search results.
  6. Close the Navigation Pane with Escape.
  7. Press Ctrl+H.
  8. Replace one occurrence of computer with technology.
  9. Do not use Replace All.
  10. Save the file as Word-Lesson-3.docx.
  11. Press Ctrl+W to close the document.
  12. Press Ctrl+O.
  13. Locate Word-Lesson-3.docx and reopen it.
  14. Press Ctrl+Home and verify the document contents.

Your Assignment

Submit Word-Lesson-3.docx.

Before Submitting

  • The document should contain three paragraphs.
  • At least one occurrence of computer should be changed to technology.
  • The document should have been successfully saved and reopened.
