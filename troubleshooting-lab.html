Troubleshooting habits

  1. Pause before repeating a command.
  2. Confirm the active application and keyboard focus.
  3. Identify the current screen-reader or interaction mode.
  4. Try a reversible check before restarting or closing anything.
  5. Protect unsaved work and private information.
