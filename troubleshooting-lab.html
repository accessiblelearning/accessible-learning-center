Read command-practice safety information

While the blue practice area has focus, Mission Control blocks the included practice commands from reaching the webpage. It cannot block operating-system or protected browser commands everywhere.

Commands such as Alt+Tab, Windows+E, Windows+D, Ctrl+Alt+Delete, Ctrl+W, and F5 are learn-only. Do not physically practice those commands here because they could switch, close, reload, or otherwise affect your computer. A running screen reader may also intercept its own commands before this page receives them.
