PowerPoint Lesson 9

Speaker Notes and Presenting with the Keyboard

PowerPoint manual All PowerPoint lessons My progress

Goal

Prepare concise speaker notes and deliver a presentation using keyboard commands.

Skills practiced

  • Add speaker notes.
  • Start a slide show from the beginning or current slide.
  • Move through slides and end the show.
  • Provide spoken descriptions for important visuals.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open PowerPoint-Lesson-8.pptx.
  2. Press F6 until JAWS announces the Notes pane for the current slide.
  3. Add two or three short speaker-note prompts. Do not paste the entire slide into the notes.
  4. Add notes to every content slide.
  5. For slides with meaningful images or charts, include a prompt to describe the important information aloud.
  6. Press F5 to start the slide show from the beginning.
  7. Use Right Arrow, Spacebar, or Page Down to move forward.
  8. Use Left Arrow, Backspace, or Page Up to move backward.
  9. Press a slide number followed by Enter to move directly to that slide when needed.
  10. Press B to display a black screen during a pause, then press B again to return.
  11. Press Escape to end the slide show.
  12. Save the presentation as PowerPoint-Lesson-9.pptx.

Keyboard help: Press Alt+Q to search PowerPoint commands when Ribbon KeyTips differ. For JAWS help, press Insert+Spacebar, then J, and search for the task.
