Focus 40 Blue Lesson 1

Orientation, Power, and Device Identification

Focus 40 Blue manualAll Focus lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create a tactile orientation guide naming the major controls and explaining how to identify the device generation.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Place the Focus on a stable surface with the braille cells nearest you.
  2. Locate the 40 braille cells, cursor router buttons, Perkins-style keys, Spacebar, Shift buttons, panning buttons, NAV rockers, rocker bars, selector buttons, and Menu button by touch.
  3. Use the centered Menu button to identify a fifth-generation model; older Focus models have different controls and firmware.
  4. When operating on battery, hold Power for about two seconds to turn the display on.
  5. Press Menu to review battery status, then return without changing settings.
  6. Hold Power for about three and one-half seconds to turn off when not connected by USB.

JAWS and braille tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Focus commands vary by device generation, JAWS settings, application, and connection method; use the official guide linked from the manual when a command differs.
