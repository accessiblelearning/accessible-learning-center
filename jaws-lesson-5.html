Screen Readers Lesson 5

Browse Webpages by Headings, Links, Lists, and Landmarks

Screen Readers manualAll Screen Readers lessonsMy progress

Choose your screen reader

Complete this lesson with JAWS, Windows Narrator, or NVDA. Use one primary screen reader at a time. Follow the column for your selection whenever commands differ.

Goal

Navigate structured webpages efficiently with the selected screen reader.

Shared practice steps

  1. Open Chrome and visit the Accessible Learning Center.
  2. Move to the top with Ctrl+Home.
  3. Turn on or confirm the screen reader’s web-reading mode.
  4. Press H and Shift+H to move among headings.
  5. Press K and Shift+K to move among links where supported; Tab also moves through interactive controls.
  6. Navigate a list and landmark or region, then return to the page heading.
