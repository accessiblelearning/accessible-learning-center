Screen Readers Lesson 6

Complete Forms and Manage Input Modes

Screen Readers manualAll Screen Readers lessonsMy progress

Choose your screen reader

Complete this lesson with JAWS, Windows Narrator, or NVDA. Use one primary screen reader at a time. Follow the column for your selection whenever commands differ.

Goal

Enter fictional information in web forms while controlling reading and typing modes.

Shared practice steps

  1. Open an instructor-approved practice form.
  2. Navigate to the first field with F or Tab.
  3. Listen for the label, field type, required state, and instructions.
  4. Enter fictional data only.
  5. Move through radio buttons, checkboxes, lists, and buttons using standard form controls.
  6. Review all entries before leaving the page without submitting to an external organization.

Your assignment

Document a fictional form practice, including how your screen reader enters and leaves its typing mode.

Use fictional, public, instructor-provided, or redacted information only. Never submit passwords, license information, account codes, or private workplace data.

Before submitting

  • The selected screen reader is named.
  • The commands match JAWS, Narrator, or NVDA.
  • Shared Windows commands are distinguished from screen-reader commands.
  • The file contains no credentials or sensitive information.
