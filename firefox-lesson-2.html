Open a page and find text

What you will learn: Separate the address bar from page content.

Exactly how to do it

  1. In Firefox on Windows, press Ctrl+L. Type accessiblelearningcenter.org and press Enter.
  2. Use Tab to explore links. Activate Manuals with Enter.
  3. Press Ctrl+F, type Thunderbird, then press Escape to close Find.

What should happen: The manual title is located on the page.

If that does not happen: If you typed into the wrong control, return to the address bar and identify the page before retrying.

Practice: Find a topic you know. Explain the difference between searching this page and searching the entire web.

Source: Mozilla keyboard shortcuts.
