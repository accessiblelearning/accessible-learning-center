Google Calendar Lesson 9

Sharing, Privacy, and Time Zones

Google Calendar manualAll Google Calendar lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create a privacy and time-zone plan for a remote team.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open Settings for a practice calendar.
  2. Review access permissions without changing them.
  3. Keep event details private unless sharing is required.
  4. Review the primary and secondary time-zone settings.
  5. Compare how one fictional meeting appears in two time zones.
  6. Close settings without exposing real calendar data.
