Accessible PDFs Lesson 7

Scanned PDFs and OCR

Accessible PDFs manualAll Accessible PDFs lessonsMy progress

Goal

Compare a scanned page before and after OCR, or describe the required workflow.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open a known scanned practice page.
  2. Try Ctrl+F to determine whether searchable text exists.
  3. Use Acrobat OCR only if the installed edition provides it.
  4. Choose the correct document language before recognition.
  5. Review OCR output for names, numbers, and headings.
  6. Keep the original scan unchanged.

JAWS and keyboard tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Commands can vary by software version and device model; use the official manual linked from the course manual when a control differs.
