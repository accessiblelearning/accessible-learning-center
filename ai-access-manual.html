Responsible AI access skills

NotebookLM, Be My Eyes, and AI-Assisted Access

Use AI summaries and image descriptions as evidence to verify, not facts to trust automatically.

Navigate an AI tool

Keyboard commands: Tab and Shift+Tab move among controls. Screen-reader heading, button, edit-field, and landmark commands help inspect the page.

  1. Confirm the product and active account.
  2. Locate the prompt, source, or image control by its accessible name.
  3. Enter only public, fictional, or authorized information.
  4. Read the complete response before acting on it.

What you should hear: A clearly named input control and a structured response.

If that does not happen: Check headings and landmarks, then confirm whether focus moved after the response appeared.

Verify a NotebookLM answer

Keyboard approach: Use standard browser and screen-reader navigation to move between the answer and its cited sources.

  1. Ask one specific question about an authorized source.
  2. Locate each citation supplied with the answer.
  3. Open the source passage and compare the wording and context.
  4. Record omissions or claims not supported by the source.

If that does not happen: Treat the answer as unverified. Search the original source directly.

Use an AI image description safely

Keyboard approach: Locate the image-description or upload control by its current accessible name.

  1. Do not submit faces, identification, medical information, credentials, or private documents without authorization.
  2. Ask the tool to separate visible details from interpretation.
  3. Verify text, numbers, colors, orientation, and safety-critical details another way.
  4. Use a trusted person when an error could cause harm.

If that does not happen: State what remains uncertain rather than guessing.
