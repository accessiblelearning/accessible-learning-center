Goal

Plan a four-week fictional training series.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Create a new event and open the repeat control.
  2. Choose a weekly pattern with a clear end date.
  3. Review the summary before saving.
  4. Edit one occurrence and compare it with editing the entire series.
  5. Cancel without changing real events if the distinction is unclear.
  6. Record the safe choice for each scenario.
