Address bar and browser controls

  • Ctrl+L: address bar.
  • Alt+Left Arrow and Alt+Right Arrow: Back and Forward.
  • Ctrl+R: reload.
  • Esc: stop loading or dismiss a control.
  • F6: cycle through the webpage and browser areas.
  • Ctrl+F: find text on the current page.
  • Ctrl+Plus, Ctrl+Minus, Ctrl+0: zoom in, out, and reset.

Tabs and windows

  • Ctrl+T: new tab.
  • Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab: next and previous tab.
  • Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+8: numbered tab; Ctrl+9: last tab.
  • Ctrl+W: close active tab.
  • Ctrl+Shift+T: reopen last closed tab.
  • Ctrl+N: new window.
  • Alt+Tab: switch applications or windows.

Confirm the active tab or window before closing it.

Chrome with JAWS

JAWS normally uses the Virtual Cursor on webpages. H moves by heading, P by paragraph, L by list, I by list item, T by table, and F by form field. Insert+F6 opens headings, Insert+F7 links, and Insert+F5 form fields. Insert+Ctrl+F performs JAWS Find; Ctrl+F performs Chrome Find.

Press Enter on a field to use Forms Mode and Num Pad Plus to leave it in the JAWS desktop layout.

Search and reliable research

Use specific search terms. Check the organization, author or publisher, date, web address, and purpose. Prefer primary and authoritative sources for factual instructions, and confirm important facts with a second reliable source.

Bookmarks, history, and downloads

  • Ctrl+D: bookmark current page.
  • Ctrl+Shift+O: Bookmark Manager.
  • Ctrl+H: History.
  • Ctrl+J: Downloads.

Verify a download’s source, filename, extension, and location before opening it. Do not clear shared history or browsing data without permission.

Uploads and forms

Before uploading, confirm the selected filename and ensure it contains no private client or workplace information. On forms, read labels and required-field messages. Review permission requests for notifications, camera, microphone, and location based on the site and task.

Accessibility and zoom

Chrome zoom changes webpage size; Windows and assistive-technology settings affect the wider system. Test pages at 200 percent zoom and ensure headings, links, controls, and content remain usable. Website controls complement native browser, screen-reader, and device settings.

Privacy and Safe Browsing

  • Keep Safe Browsing enabled.
  • Do not bypass deceptive-site or dangerous-download warnings.
  • Grant site permissions only when the trusted site needs them for the current task.
  • Incognito limits local history but does not hide activity from websites, employers, schools, or internet providers.
  • Managed workplace settings may require administrator approval.
