Accessible Job Search Lesson 7

Write a Targeted Cover Letter

Accessible Job Search manualAll Accessible Job Search lessonsMy progress

Goal

Create a fictional or redacted targeted cover letter.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Address the role and employer specifically.
  2. Open with the job title and reason for interest.
  3. Connect two qualifications to stated job needs.
  4. Use short paragraphs and plain language.
  5. Close with a professional call to action.
  6. Proofread with JAWS and spelling tools.

JAWS and keyboard tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Commands can vary by software version and device model; use the official manual linked from the course manual when a control differs.

Your assignment

Create a fictional or redacted targeted cover letter.

Use fictional, public, or redacted information only. Never submit passwords, account codes, private NLS books, financial details, or other sensitive information.

Save your work with a clear filename beginning Job-search-Lesson-7.

Before submitting

  • All requested steps are complete.
  • The file contains no private credentials or sensitive personal information.
  • The content is organized with clear headings or labels.
  • The filename identifies Accessible Job Search Lesson 7.
