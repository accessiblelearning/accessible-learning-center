Official NLS Braille eReader Zoomax User Guide

Purpose: This manual is written directly to the person learning the NLS Braille eReader model EMZ-20. It assumes you may be touching the device for the first time. Every procedure identifies exactly where a button or port is located, what to press, what should appear on the 20-cell braille display, and what to do if the result is different.

Device identity: The EMZ-20 is the Zoomax version of the NLS Braille eReader. NLS also distributes a different HumanWare 20-cell eReader. The button layout and commands are not interchangeable.

Official reference: This manual is original training material based on the current NLS Braille eReader Zoomax User Guide and Getting Started information from the National Library Service for the Blind and Print Disabled, Library of Congress.

How to Use This Manual

  • Begin with Parts 1 through 7 even if you already know braille. They teach the physical layout and command system used throughout the rest of the device.

  • Orient the device with the 20 braille cells closest to your body unless a procedure says otherwise.

  • Dot 7 is the Backspace key. Dot 8 is the Enter key.

  • The six Display keys are D1, D2, D3 on the left and D4, D5, D6 on the right.

  • The four System keys are S1 and S2 on the far-left front area, and S3 and S4 on the far-right front area.

  • The two large rectangular front keys are BL and BR. They act like Space for many commands and act as panning keys while reading a book.

  • The five center navigation keys are N-Left, N-Up, N-Right, N-Down, and N-Center.

  • Do one action, then stop and read the display before pressing another key.

  • When you forget a command, use S3 to open the Context Menu or open Help from the Main Menu.

Part 1 - Identify the EMZ-20 and What It Does

The EMZ-20 is the Zoomax NLS Braille eReader. It is a 20-cell refreshable braille display and standalone electronic braille reader distributed through NLS network libraries. It can read electronic braille and other supported documents, download NLS content through BARD, use external storage, and function as a braille display for compatible computers and Apple mobile devices.

  • 20 refreshable braille cells with a routing button behind each cell.

  • Eight-key Perkins-style braille keyboard: dots 1 through 8.

  • Three Space-key locations: the center Space key plus BL and BR for many chord commands.

  • Six Display keys: D1 through D6.

  • Four System keys: S1 through S4.

  • Five Navigation keys: N-Left, N-Up, N-Right, N-Down, and N-Center.

  • USB-C for charging and computer connection.

  • USB-A for NLS cartridge cable or USB flash drive.

  • SD-card slot for external storage.

  • Wi-Fi for BARD and NFB-NEWSLINE.

  • Bluetooth for compatible external screen-reader devices.

Part 2 - Physical Layout: Top Surface

2.1 Orient the Device Correctly

What you will learn: Place the EMZ-20 in the correct position before learning any buttons.

Before you begin: Place the device on a stable surface.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Find the long row of 20 refreshable braille cells. Rotate the device until those cells are closest to your body.

  2. Keep the device in that orientation for all physical descriptions in this manual.

  3. Place both index fingers lightly on the braille cells and move from the leftmost cell to the rightmost cell.

  4. Move your fingertips slightly toward the back of the device. Directly behind each braille cell is a small round cursor-routing button.

  5. Move farther toward the back to locate the Perkins-style braille keyboard.

What should happen: The braille cells are along the front/top edge closest to you, routing buttons are immediately behind them, and the braille keyboard is farther back.

If that does not happen: If you cannot identify the row of 20 cells, do not press keys. Rotate the unit until the long line of braille cells is nearest you.

Practice: With the unit powered off, find cells, routing buttons, and keyboard three times.

2.2 Learn the Perkins Keyboard by Touch

Layout: From the center toward the left are dots 1, 2, 3, and dot 7. From the center toward the right are dots 4, 5, 6, and dot 8. Dot 7 also serves as Backspace; dot 8 also serves as Enter. The Space key is centered below/between the braille input groups.

  • Left-hand group from inner to outer: dot 1, dot 2, dot 3, dot 7.

  • Right-hand group from inner to outer: dot 4, dot 5, dot 6, dot 8.

  • Dot 7 = Backspace.

  • Dot 8 = Enter.

  • Center Space is used by itself and in chord commands.

Part 3 - Physical Layout: Front Controls

Along the angled front panel closest to you are eleven controls. Learn them by moving from the outside edges toward the center.

Keyboard lock: Pressing S1 and S4 together toggles keyboard lock. When locked, the display reads keyboard locked. Press S1+S4 again, or use the Power On button as described by NLS, to unlock.

Part 4 - Physical Layout: Left and Right Sides

4.1 Left Side

With the braille cells closest to you, feel along the LEFT SIDE from front to back.

  • Front corner: lanyard attachment point.

  • Behind the lanyard point: SD-card slot.

  • The braille letters “sd” are above the SD-card slot.

4.2 Right Side

With the braille cells closest to you, feel along the RIGHT SIDE from front to back. The order is critical because this is where power and charging are located.

  • Front corner: lanyard attachment point.

  • Immediately behind it: small round POWER ON button. Braille “on” is above it.

  • Immediately behind Power On: small round POWER OFF button. Braille “off” is above it.

  • Behind the power buttons: USB-C port. This is the charging port and the port used for USB computer connection.

  • Farthest toward the back: USB-A port. This accepts a USB flash drive or the NLS cartridge cable.

  • Braille “usb” is centered over the two USB ports, with “c” over USB-C and “a” over USB-A.

Back edge: The back edge has no buttons or ports.

Part 5 - Charging the Battery

5.1 Charge the EMZ-20

What you will learn: Connect the correct cable to the correct port.

Before you begin: Orient the device with the braille cells closest to you. Find the RIGHT side.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Feel from the front-right lanyard point toward the back: Power On, Power Off, then USB-C. Stop at USB-C. It is directly behind the two power buttons.

  2. Take the smaller USB-C end of the supplied charging cable.

  3. Insert it into the USB-C port. USB-C is reversible, so either orientation is acceptable.

  4. Connect the larger USB-A end to the supplied AC adapter.

  5. Plug the AC adapter into a wall outlet.

  6. Allow about three hours for a full charge when needed.

  7. The original Zoomax guide describes roughly ten hours of use per charge; actual battery life varies with settings, battery age, wireless use, and power-saving behavior.

What should happen: The eReader charges through the USB-C port on the right side behind the Power Off button.

If that does not happen: If the plug does not enter easily, stop and make sure you are at USB-C rather than the USB-A port. Never force the connector. Try the supplied adapter/cable and another outlet if charging does not begin.

Practice: Locate USB-C correctly three times before connecting power.

Part 6 - Power On, Power Off, and Suspend

6.1 Turn the EMZ-20 On

What you will learn: Start the device and recognize the startup sequence.

Before you begin: Charge the eReader first.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Orient the device with braille cells closest to you.

  2. On the RIGHT side, start at the front lanyard point and move slightly toward the back to the first small round button. This is Power On.

  3. Press the Power On button once.

  4. Listen for three ascending tones.

  5. Read the display. “starting” appears briefly, followed by “NLS eReader Zoomax,” then startup progress.

  6. Wait. Do not press navigation keys while startup is still occurring.

  7. When startup finishes, you hear another ascending tone sequence and the display reaches the Main Menu, normally at Book Reader.

What should happen: Book Reader or the top Main Menu item appears and the eReader is ready.

If that does not happen: If nothing happens, connect power and try Power On again. Make sure you pressed the FIRST of the two small round power buttons, not Power Off.

Practice: Power the unit on and identify when startup is complete.

6.2 Turn the EMZ-20 Off

What you will learn: Shut down without confusing Power Off with Suspend.

Before you begin: Save/finish any current task first.

Exactly how to do it

  1. On the RIGHT side, find the two small round power buttons. Power Off is the SECOND one, directly behind Power On.

  2. Press Power Off once.

  3. Listen for descending tones and read the Off menu.

  4. Move to Power Off if it is not already selected.

  5. Press Enter (dot 8), N-Center, or the routing button corresponding to the Power Off choice as appropriate.

  6. Wait while shutdown progress appears. Do not disconnect power or press other keys until shutdown is complete.

What should happen: The display goes blank after the shutdown sequence.

If that does not happen: If the device enters Suspend instead, wake it with Power On and repeat, carefully selecting Power Off from the Off menu.

Practice: Practice locating Power On and Power Off by touch while the device is already off.

6.3 Suspend Mode

Suspend uses less power and lets you resume quickly. The eReader can enter Suspend after inactivity according to its settings. You can also select Suspend from the Main Menu or from the menu opened with the Power Off button. Wake from Suspend by pressing Power On.

Part 8 - Main Menu

The current Zoomax NLS guide organizes the main functions around these primary areas. Firmware revisions can rename or reorganize an item, so use the built-in Help/User Guide if your menu wording differs.

  • Book Reader

  • Online Services (BARD and NFB-NEWSLINE)

  • File Manager

  • Settings

  • Device Info

  • Battery

  • Help

  • Power Off

8.1 Return to the Main Menu from Anywhere

What you will learn: Use a reliable “home” command when you get lost.

Before you begin: The device is on.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press S1, the far-left outer small round System key on the front panel.

  2. Alternatively, hold Space and press the braille letter m (dots 1-3-4).

  3. Read the menu item now shown.

  4. Use N-Up/N-Down to identify nearby Main Menu items.

What should happen: You return to the Main Menu.

If that does not happen: If the display reads keyboard locked, press S1+S4 together to unlock, then press S1 again.

Practice: From Book Reader, Settings, and File Manager, return to Main Menu.

Part 9 - Menu Navigation and First-Letter Navigation

9.1 Move Through Menus

What you will learn: Choose menu items deliberately.

Before you begin: Be on any menu.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press N-Down once to move to the next item. You can also use D3.

  2. Stop and read the 20-cell display.

  3. Press N-Down again only after identifying the current item.

  4. Press N-Up to move to the previous item.

  5. When the desired item is displayed, press Enter (dot 8), N-Center, or a cursor-routing button over the item where supported.

What should happen: The selected menu/submenu opens.

If that does not happen: If the wrong item opens, press Space+e to go back and repeat one item at a time.

Practice: Open Settings and return to Main Menu.

9.2 Use First-Letter Navigation

What you will learn: Jump to an item without stepping through every menu entry.

Before you begin: Be in a menu or list that supports first-letter navigation.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Type the first braille letter of the item you want.

  2. Read the item that appears.

  3. If another item begins with the same letter, type the same letter again to move to the next matching item.

  4. Press Enter on the correct item.

What should happen: The display jumps to an item beginning with that letter.

If that does not happen: Some book lists use first-letter typing as a filter rather than simply jumping. Use Backspace (dot 7) to clear the filter in BARD lists.

Practice: From Main Menu, use the first letter for Settings or File Manager.

Part 10 - Context Menu and Global Commands

10.1 Use the Context Menu

What you will learn: Find commands available for what you are currently doing.

Before you begin: Be in a menu, file list, book, or other application context.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press S3, the small round System key second from the far-right side of the front panel.

  2. Read the first Context Menu item.

  3. Use N-Up/N-Down through actions.

  4. Press Enter or a routing button on the desired action.

  5. To cancel, press Space+e (dots 1-5).

What should happen: A context-specific action runs or the menu closes.

If that does not happen: If S3 does not give the command you expected, the available actions may differ in the current application. Use S2 for that application’s main menu or Help for command lists.

Practice: Open S3 in Book Reader and File Manager and compare the options.

Part 11 - Reading 20-Cell Braille and Panning

11.1 Pan Through Text Longer Than 20 Cells

What you will learn: Read a line or paragraph in segments.

Before you begin: Open a book or document.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Read the current 20 cells from left to right.

  2. To move forward, press D5, N-Right, or BR while reading a book.

  3. Read the next displayed segment.

  4. To move backward, press D2, N-Left, or BL while reading a book.

  5. Repeat until you have read the passage.

What should happen: The display advances or retreats through the text.

If that does not happen: If the keys move a menu selection rather than the text, you are probably not inside an open book/document.

Practice: Read a passage requiring at least four pans and return to its beginning.

Part 12 - Book Reader Basics

12.1 Open Book Reader

What you will learn: Open the built-in reading application.

Before you begin: Be at Main Menu.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Use N-Up/N-Down until Book Reader appears, or type b until Book Reader is selected.

  2. Press Enter.

  3. Read the Book Reader menu.

  4. Choose Current Book to resume the last book, or Book List to choose another title.

  5. Press S4 from almost anywhere when you want to return directly to the current book at your last reading position.

What should happen: A book opens or the Book Reader list appears.

If that does not happen: If Current Book says the book is empty, open Book List and choose an available title.

Practice: Open a book, exit, then reopen it with S4.

Part 14 - Where Am I, Autoscroll, Word Wrap, and Reflow

14.1 Find Your Position in a Book

What you will learn: Ask the eReader where you are.

Before you begin: Be inside an open book.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press the braille “wh” sign, dots 1-5-6.

  2. Read the position information.

  3. Note the volume, page, line, and character information that is available.

  4. Dismiss the message with Enter/N-Center if needed.

What should happen: Position information appears.

If that does not happen: If the command does not work, verify that the book has focus and consult Help > Keyboard Commands for your firmware.

Practice: Check your position before and after moving several pages.

14.2 Use Autoscroll

What you will learn: Read without manually pressing a pan key each display.

Before you begin: Autoscroll must be enabled in Book Reader Settings.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Inside a book, press the braille st-sign, dots 3-4.

  2. Read while the display advances automatically.

  3. To slow the rate, press dots 3-4 with dot 7.

  4. To increase the rate, press dots 3-4 with dot 8.

  5. Press dots 3-4 again to stop Autoscroll.

What should happen: The display advances automatically at the selected speed.

If that does not happen: If nothing happens, open Settings > Book Reader Settings and make sure Autoscroll is on.

Practice: Start Autoscroll, change speed once, then stop it.

Word Wrap and Reflow: Word Wrap prevents ordinary words from being split across the 20-cell display. The documented Word Wrap toggle is w-chord (dots 2-4-5-6). Book reflow can also be enabled in Book Reader Settings to improve 20-cell presentation.

Part 15 - BARD: Wi-Fi and First Login

Before BARD can be used directly from the eReader, the device must have a working Wi-Fi connection and permanent BARD credentials.

15.1 Open NLS BARD

What you will learn: Reach the BARD application.

Before you begin: Connect to Wi-Fi first.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press S1 for Main Menu.

  2. Navigate to Online Services, or type o (dots 1-3-5).

  3. Press Enter.

  4. Navigate to NLS BARD.

  5. Press Enter.

What should happen: The NLS BARD menu opens.

If that does not happen: If BARD cannot access online content, confirm Wi-Fi connection under Settings > Wi-Fi Settings.

Practice: Open BARD and return to Online Services.

15.2 Log in to BARD the First Time

What you will learn: Enter your BARD credentials correctly in braille.

Before you begin: You need a permanent BARD username/password; temporary passwords must be changed through a web browser first.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Open NLS BARD.

  2. Choose User login if you are not automatically prompted.

  3. Enter your BARD username in uncontracted UEB unless you intentionally select another input code from the Context Menu.

  4. Enter your permanent BARD password.

  5. For email addresses in UEB, enter the at-sign and period using correct UEB symbols; numbers require a number sign.

  6. If you prefer computer braille or another available input mode, press S3 and choose the desired code before entering credentials.

  7. Confirm the login.

What should happen: A successful login message appears and future BARD sessions can log in automatically.

If that does not happen: If login fails, verify the credentials on another device, make sure the password is permanent, and confirm your braille input code.

Practice: Open the User Login area and identify the fields without changing a working login.

Part 16 - BARD: Browse, Search, Download, and Wish List

The BARD menu includes Browse BARD, Search BARD, Wish list, User login, and Exit.

16.1 Download and Open a Book from BARD

What you will learn: Find a title and begin reading it.

Before you begin: Wi-Fi and BARD login must be working.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Open BARD.

  2. Choose Browse BARD or Search BARD.

  3. If browsing, choose Most Recent Books, Most Popular Books, Magazine Issues, or Previous Downloads.

  4. If searching, choose Book Title, Author Name, Subjects, or Keyword.

  5. Navigate the result list. In BARD lists, typing letters filters titles; press Backspace (dot 7) to clear the typed filter.

  6. Press Enter on a title for more information.

  7. Read the title, author, and description as desired.

  8. Navigate to Download and Open if you want to read immediately, or Download if you want to continue browsing.

  9. Press Enter.

  10. Wait for the download to complete.

What should happen: The title is added to your Book List; Download and Open also starts the book in Book Reader.

If that does not happen: If download fails, verify Wi-Fi, BARD login, and available device storage.

Practice: Search for a known title and reach the information screen without downloading unless desired.

Wish List: From a book information screen or Context Menu, use Add to Wish list. The Wish list area lets you review, download, and remove saved items.

Part 17 - NFB-NEWSLINE

17.1 Sign In and Download NEWSLINE Material

What you will learn: Use an NFB-NEWSLINE account directly from the eReader.

Before you begin: You need Wi-Fi plus your NFB-NEWSLINE ID and security code.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press S1 for Main Menu.

  2. Open Online Services.

  3. Open NFB Newsline.

  4. The first time, enter your numeric Newsline ID preceded by the braille number sign.

  5. Enter the numeric security code the same way.

  6. Press Enter and wait for “Login successful.”

  7. Open Publication Management.

  8. Choose Sync Now to download the latest issues for subscribed/Favorite publications.

  9. After downloading, return to Book Reader > Book List to read the publications.

What should happen: Newsline material downloads and appears in Book Reader.

If that does not happen: If login fails, confirm ID/security code and Wi-Fi.

Practice: Open Publication Management and identify Sync Now.

Reading Newsline: NLS states that Newsline articles are usually navigation level 3 and sections usually level 2. While reading, use N-Up/N-Down to select a navigation level and N-Left/N-Right to move to the previous/next item at that level.

Part 18 - File Manager

18.1 Open and Browse File Manager

What you will learn: Find internal, USB, and SD files.

Before you begin: Be at Main Menu.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Navigate to File Manager or type f (dots 1-2-4).

  2. Press Enter.

  3. The internal files area is always available.

  4. Use N-Up/N-Down through storage locations. USB files and SD Card files appear only when connected.

  5. Press Enter on a storage location.

  6. Use N-Up/N-Down through folders/files.

  7. Folder names have a slash symbol before them.

  8. Press Enter on a folder to open it.

  9. Use N-Left and N-Right to navigate the folder hierarchy.

What should happen: You can browse internal and attached storage.

If that does not happen: If USB or SD does not appear, remove and reconnect it and wait for recognition.

Practice: Open internal files, enter a folder, and return.

18.2 File Manager Menus

  • Press S2 for File Manager Menu: Sort by Name, Type, Date New First, Size, or Create Directory.

  • Press S3 for file Context Menu: Open, Cut, Copy, Paste, Rename, Delete, File Info, Select, Unselect, Toggle selection, Select All, and Unselect All.

Part 19 - NLS Cartridge, USB Flash Drive, and SD Card

19.1 Copy an NLS Cartridge Automatically

What you will learn: Load braille books from an NLS cartridge.

Before you begin: Use the supplied NLS cartridge-to-USB cable.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Orient the eReader with braille cells closest to you.

  2. On the RIGHT side, locate the farthest-back port: USB-A.

  3. Connect the NLS cartridge to the cartridge cable.

  4. Connect the cable to the EMZ-20 USB-A port.

  5. Wait for the recognition tones.

  6. Wait for the progress indicator and “auto copy complete.”

  7. Open Book Reader > Book List and locate the copied books.

What should happen: The cartridge books are copied to internal storage and appear in Book List.

If that does not happen: If Powersave is on, NLS notes Auto Copy works only while you are in File Manager or Book List. Move there and reconnect the cartridge if needed.

Practice: Locate USB-A by touch without confusing it with USB-C.

A USB flash drive connects directly to USB-A. An SD card inserts into the SD-card slot on the LEFT side behind the lanyard attachment point. Use File Manager to browse these devices.

Part 20 - Settings

Open Settings from Main Menu by moving to Settings or typing s (dots 2-3-4), then press Enter.

  • Book Reader Settings: condense blank lines, formatting, spaces, line-down behavior, Autoscroll, book contraction, reflow, and related reading options.

  • File Manager Settings: includes delete confirmation.

  • Braille Settings: braille display/input choices.

  • Device Settings: device-wide behavior such as word wrap and power settings.

  • Wi-Fi Settings.

  • Bluetooth Mode.

  • USB Mode.

  • Drives for Reader / Drives for Computer.

  • Restore Defaults.

Saving settings: For list-style settings, NLS instructs users to press Space to cycle options, then navigate to the bottom and press Enter on OK to save.

Part 21 - Braille Settings and Grade

The eReader can display content in contracted or uncontracted braille according to settings and document type. The global command Space+g (dots 1-2-4-5) switches braille grade in supported contexts.

21.1 Change Braille Grade

What you will learn: Switch between available braille presentation modes.

Before you begin: Open a book or text where grade switching is supported.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Hold Space.

  2. Press dots 1-2-4-5 (the braille letter g).

  3. Release the keys.

  4. Read the status change/message.

  5. Read a familiar sentence to confirm the result.

What should happen: Braille presentation changes between available grade settings.

If that does not happen: If the content is already a prepared BRF, translation behavior can differ. Review Braille Settings and the file type.

Practice: Switch grade and restore your preferred setting.

Part 22 - Wi-Fi

22.1 Connect to a Visible Wi-Fi Network

What you will learn: Join a wireless network directly from the eReader.

Before you begin: Know the exact network name and password.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press S1 for Main Menu.

  2. Open Settings.

  3. Open Wi-Fi Settings.

  4. Confirm Wi-Fi is on. Press Enter on the Wi-Fi on/off item if needed.

  5. Open Visible Wi-Fi Networks.

  6. Use N-Up/N-Down until your exact network name appears.

  7. Press Enter.

  8. Press Enter on Join.

  9. Enter the password in uncontracted UEB unless you deliberately choose another input code through S3.

  10. Navigate to OK and press Enter.

  11. Wait for the connection result.

What should happen: The eReader connects to the selected network.

If that does not happen: If connection fails, check password capitalization, UEB symbols/numbers, network signal, and input braille code.

Practice: Open Known Wi-Fi Networks after connecting and confirm the network is present.

22.2 Optional autowifi.txt Method

NLS documents an alternate method: create a plain text file named autowifi.txt with the network name on line 1 and password on line 2, place it in the root of a USB flash drive or SD card, then insert it while the eReader is on. This exposes the Wi-Fi password in plain text on that media, so protect or delete the file afterward.

Part 24 - Bluetooth Mode

24.1 Put the EMZ-20 into Bluetooth Mode

What you will learn: Make the eReader ready to pair with another device.

Before you begin: The eReader is on.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press S1 for Main Menu.

  2. Open Settings.

  3. Navigate to Bluetooth Mode.

  4. Press Enter.

  5. The display should read BT.

  6. Alternative: tap the Power On button and activate the routing button over BT when that quick mode menu is presented.

  7. To toggle Bluetooth on or off globally, use Space+t (dots 2-3-4-5) as documented by NLS.

What should happen: BT appears and the eReader is ready for Bluetooth connection/pairing.

Part 25 - Connect to iPhone or iPad

25.1 Pair EMZ-20 with VoiceOver

What you will learn: Use the eReader as an iPhone/iPad braille display and braille keyboard.

Before you begin: VoiceOver should be enabled on the iPhone/iPad.

Exactly how to do it

  1. EMZ-20: Enter Bluetooth Mode. The display should read BT.

  2. iPhone/iPad: Open Settings.

  3. Open Accessibility.

  4. Open VoiceOver.

  5. Open Braille.

  6. Move to the bottom area labeled Choose a Braille Display.

  7. Find “NLS eReader Z” followed by the last five digits of your eReader serial number. VoiceOver should say it is not paired.

  8. Double-tap the NLS eReader entry.

  9. When prompted for the PIN, enter 1111 on the iPhone/iPad.

  10. Double-tap Pair.

  11. Wait for pairing.

  12. Move VoiceOver focus on the iPhone/iPad and verify the EMZ-20 braille changes.

  13. Open an editable field and test braille keyboard input.

  14. Press a cursor-routing button and confirm VoiceOver/caret movement where supported.

What should happen: iPhone/iPad content appears on the 20-cell display and braille keyboard input works.

If that does not happen: If the device does not appear or pair, NLS recommends powering off both devices, turning the eReader back on, then the iOS device, and repeating the process. If paired but braille is blank, touch/move focus on the iOS screen.

Practice: Perform the three tests: output, input, routing.

Part 26 - Connect to Mac with VoiceOver

26.1 Pair EMZ-20 with a Mac

What you will learn: Use the EMZ-20 as a VoiceOver braille display over Bluetooth.

Before you begin: VoiceOver is running on the Mac.

Exactly how to do it

  1. EMZ-20: Enter Bluetooth Mode.

  2. Mac: Open VoiceOver Utility. The NLS guide describes VO+F8; depending on current Mac function-key settings, Fn may also be needed.

  3. In VoiceOver Utility, select Braille.

  4. Move to Displays and activate it.

  5. Move to Add and activate it.

  6. Find “NLS eReader Zoomax” followed by the eReader serial number in the Bluetooth devices table.

  7. Interact with the eReader row using VoiceOver interaction commands.

  8. Move to Pair and activate it.

  9. When prompted, enter 1111 and press Return.

  10. Press Escape to leave device selection.

  11. Close VoiceOver Utility when finished.

  12. Test braille output with VoiceOver navigation, then test input and routing.

What should happen: VoiceOver content appears on the EMZ-20 and supported commands/input work.

If that does not happen: If the Mac cannot find the eReader, confirm Bluetooth Mode and restart Bluetooth/device pairing. If it pairs but no braille appears, verify the display is added in VoiceOver Utility > Braille.

Practice: Navigate several Mac controls and confirm the braille display follows VoiceOver.

Part 29 - Keyboard Lock and Common Recovery Skills

29.1 Unlock the Keyboard

What you will learn: Recover when the display says keyboard locked.

Before you begin: The display reads keyboard locked or keys do not respond normally.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Locate S1: far-left outer small round System key.

  2. Locate S4: far-right outer small round System key.

Part 30 - Troubleshooting

30.1 Device Will Not Turn On

What you will learn: Check power problems in the correct order.

Before you begin: Use the supplied/appropriate charger.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Confirm the charging cable is connected to USB-C on the RIGHT side behind both Power buttons.

  2. Connect the AC adapter to a known working outlet.

  3. Allow charging time if the battery may be deeply discharged.

  4. Press the first small round power button from the front: Power On.

  5. Listen for startup tones.

  6. If there is still no response, test another appropriate cable/adapter or contact your NLS network library.

What should happen: The device starts or you have isolated a likely power problem.

If that does not happen: If the battery/case becomes unusually hot, swollen, damaged, or smells unusual, stop using/charging it and contact the library/support.

Practice: Review the physical power/charging layout without connecting cables.

30.2 Wi-Fi Will Not Connect

What you will learn: Separate password/input problems from network problems.

Before you begin: Know the correct network and password.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Confirm Wi-Fi is on under Settings > Wi-Fi Settings.

  2. Confirm you selected the exact network name.

  3. Re-enter the password carefully.

  4. Confirm the braille input code used for the password.

  5. Move closer to the Wi-Fi access point if signal is weak.

  6. Try another known network if available to determine whether the problem is the eReader or the original network.

What should happen: The eReader connects or the failure is narrowed to credentials/network.

If that does not happen: If the network uses a browser/captive portal, the eReader may not be able to complete that login because it does not provide a general web browser.

Practice: Reconnect to a known network.

30.3 Paired to iPhone but No Braille Appears

What you will learn: Restore VoiceOver braille output.

Before you begin: The eReader is shown as paired.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Touch or move VoiceOver focus on the iPhone/iPad.

  2. Confirm VoiceOver is on.

  3. Return to Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver > Braille and confirm the NLS eReader is connected.

  4. Confirm the EMZ-20 is still in/available for Bluetooth display use.

  5. Toggle Bluetooth if necessary.

  6. Restart both devices and re-pair if the connection remains unusable.

What should happen: VoiceOver content appears on the EMZ-20.

If that does not happen: If another braille display is selected or the pairing record is stale, remove/re-pair according to current iOS procedures.

Practice: Test output, input, routing.

30.4 File or Book Is Missing

What you will learn: Check the correct storage and application.

Before you begin: Know where the file was copied/downloaded.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Open Book Reader > Book List for books imported through BARD or cartridge Auto Copy.

  2. Open File Manager for raw files/folders.

  3. Check internal files, USB files, and SD Card files separately.

  4. If an external device is absent, reconnect it and wait for recognition.

  5. If a file format is unsupported or packaged unexpectedly, test a known supported file.

What should happen: You find the title or identify its storage/format issue.

If that does not happen: Do not delete or recopy repeatedly until you know which storage area currently contains the file.

Practice: Locate one BARD book and one external-media file.

Part 31 - Care and Safe Handling

  • Use light fingertip pressure on refreshable braille cells.

  • Keep liquids, crumbs, and debris away from braille cells and keyboard.

  • Do not force USB-C, USB-A, or SD media into the wrong slot.

  • Use the supplied or appropriate charger.

  • Do not disassemble the battery or device.

  • Protect the unit during transport and use the lanyard only as intended.

  • Keep the Indiana/NLS property label intact if the device is loaned equipment.

  • Back up personal documents when possible; NLS library books can usually be downloaded again.
