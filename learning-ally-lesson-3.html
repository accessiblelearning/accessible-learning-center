Your goal

Find a title, verify author and edition, add it to the bookshelf, and locate it again without repeating the search.

Before starting

Use your own authorized practice account or a harmless practice document. Identify the active application and the focused control before each command. Keep these instructions in a separate window. Never include passwords or private messages in your learning notes.

Follow the product and device version named in each procedure. A plus sign means hold the modifier while pressing the final key; “then” means release before continuing. These procedures are documentation-based and have not been tested here with the actual assistive software or signed-in reading accounts.

Search, verify, and add a book to your bookshelf

Start here: The signed-in Learning Ally reading app or browser reader. Have the assigned title, author, and edition information.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Locate the app’s Search or Browse option through its labeled navigation. Activate Search and enter a distinctive title or author in the search field.
  2. Submit the search once. Read the result titles and inspect likely matches. Compare author and edition with your assignment before adding a book.
  3. Open the intended book’s details and use its add-to-bookshelf action. Confirm the action’s result before returning to the bookshelf.
  4. On the bookshelf, locate the title you added and open it. Identify whether it can be read now or whether the app is downloading content.
  5. If the assigned book is already on the shelf, open that matching edition instead of adding another similarly named title.
  6. Record the selected edition and a chapter title for your first reading task. Keep this record if your class uses printed page references.

Check your result

The correct title is on your bookshelf and you know how to open it again.

If something goes wrong

If the title is missing, confirm the account and clear any bookshelf search/filter. A book added under another account will not necessarily appear here. Ask the instructor about an unavailable edition instead of substituting silently.
