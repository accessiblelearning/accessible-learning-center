Purpose: This manual teaches the Victor Reader Stream 3 from the beginning. It assumes no prior experience with the device and gives physical orientation, exact key sequences, expected speech feedback, troubleshooting, practice, and independent-use checklists.

Reference: This guide is based on HumanWare's current Stream 3 support materials and User Guide version 1.6, with NLS/BARD information cross-checked against current NLS resources.

Terminology: This manual calls the # key Confirm and the * key Cancel, matching the device's spoken/manual terminology.

How to Use This Manual

  • Keep the Stream upright with the numeric keypad facing you and the speaker holes on the bottom edge.

  • Learn the physical layout before trying online services.

  • Use Key Describer mode when you forget a key.

  • Use key 1 to move among bookshelves and keys 4/6 to move among books on a bookshelf.

  • Use keys 2/8 to choose a navigation level and keys 4/6 to move backward/forward at that level.

  • Press Play/Stop to begin or pause reading.

  • Press key 5 for Where am I? information.

  • Press key 0 for device/book information.

  • Press and hold key 1 to open the built-in User Guide.

  • Do not enter BARD or Bookshare credentials when another person can hear them unless you are comfortable with that privacy risk.

Part 1 - What the Victor Reader Stream 3 Is

The Victor Reader Stream 3 is a pocket-size talking-book and media player from HumanWare. It supports DAISY and NLS digital talking books, electronic text, braille files, music, podcasts, online book services, Internet radio, recording, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, an SD card, and internal storage.

  • Offline bookcase: books and files stored in internal memory or on the SD card.

  • Online bookcase: Wi-Fi services such as NLS BARD, Bookshare, podcasts, radio, and other configured services.

  • Bookshelf: one category of content inside a bookcase.

  • Navigation level: chapter, heading, page, time jump, paragraph, line, word, character, or another structure supported by the current book.

  • Confirm: the # key. Cancel: the * key.

Part 2 - Physical Orientation

2.1 Front Face

  • Hold the Stream upright with the keypad facing you.

  • At the very top of the front face are three special keys. Left: square Go To key. Center: round Online key. Right: diamond-shaped Bookmark key.

  • Just above the Go To key is a tiny hole for the built-in mono microphone.

  • Below those three keys is a telephone-style 12-key numeric pad. Key 5 has two raised dots. Keys 2, 4, 6, and 8 have tactile raised edges.

  • Below the numeric pad are four playback keys. Rewind is bottom-left and has a raised edge. Fast Forward is bottom-right and has a raised edge. Play/Stop is centered between them. Sleep is directly above Play/Stop.

2.2 Left Side

  • Near the top-left side is the Power/Toggle button.

  • Directly below Power is the status LED.

  • Below the LED is a rocker-style Up/Down control. It changes whichever setting Power/Toggle has selected: volume, speed, tone, or pitch.

  • At the bottom-left side is a stereo microphone/line-in jack.

2.3 Right, Top, Bottom, and Back

  • Right side near the top: Record button, identified by a raised red circle.

  • Top edge: SD-card slot under a small silicone cover.

  • Bottom edge center: USB-C port for charging and computer connection.

  • Bottom edge left of USB-C: headphone/external-speaker jack.

  • Bottom edge right of USB-C: four small holes for the built-in speaker.

  • Back: model and serial-number label surrounded by a rubber outline.

Orientation practice: Without powering on the device, locate Go To, Online, Bookmark, 5, Play/Stop, Sleep, Power, Up/Down, Record, SD slot, USB-C, headphones, and speaker.

Part 3 - Charging, Power, and Battery

3.1 Charge the Stream

What you will learn: Connect power safely.

Before you begin: Use the supplied USB-C cable and a suitable USB power adapter.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Locate the USB-C port at the bottom center.

  2. Insert the USB-C plug without forcing it.

  3. Connect the other end to the power adapter.

  4. Plug the adapter into AC power.

  5. Leave the Stream charging. A full AC charge can take up to about three hours.

What should happen: When the player is off and charging, the LED blinks. When it is on while charging, the LED stays steady.

If that does not happen: If it does not charge, try the supplied adapter/cable and another outlet. Computer USB charging can be slower or insufficient.

Practice: Charge the device and check battery status.

3.2 Turn On or Off

What you will learn: Power the device intentionally.

Before you begin: The Stream has some battery charge.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Locate Power on the upper-left side.

  2. Press and hold it until the Stream starts.

  3. Wait for spoken startup feedback.

  4. To power off, press and hold Power again until shutdown begins.

What should happen: The Stream starts or shuts down with speech feedback.

If that does not happen: If the unit is unresponsive, connect AC power and retry. If necessary use the documented power-button reset procedure.

Practice: Turn the Stream on, identify the active bookshelf, then shut it down.

Battery status: Press and hold Confirm (#), or press Info (0), to hear battery/charging status.

Part 4 - Key Describer Mode

4.1 Learn Keys Safely

What you will learn: Have the Stream announce keys without carrying out normal actions.

Before you begin: The Stream is on.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press and hold Info (0) to enter Key Describer mode.

  2. Press any front key.

  3. Listen to its name/function.

  4. Try the side controls and playback keys.

  5. Press and hold Info (0) again to exit.

What should happen: Keys are described instead of performing their ordinary command.

If that does not happen: If the Stream performs commands, Key Describer mode was not entered; retry the long press.

Practice: Identify every key without looking at a printed guide.

Part 5 - The Bookshelf System

Previous/next book on shelf 4 / 6

5.1 Choose a Bookshelf and Book

What you will learn: Find content by category.

Before you begin: The Stream contains at least one book/file.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 1.

  2. Listen to the bookshelf name.

  3. Press 1 repeatedly until the desired bookshelf is announced.

  4. Press 4 or 6 to move backward/forward through books.

  5. Listen to each title.

  6. Press Play/Stop on the desired book.

What should happen: The selected book begins where you last stopped, when resume information is available.

If that does not happen: If a bookshelf says zero books, choose another shelf or add content.

Practice: Locate three different shelves and one title on each.

5.2 Switch Online and Offline

What you will learn: Move between local and internet content.

Before you begin: Wi-Fi may be needed for online services.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Tap the round Online key above number 2.

  2. Listen for Online or Offline bookcase.

  3. Press 1 to hear the first bookshelf in that bookcase.

  4. Tap Online again to return.

What should happen: The bookcase changes while the same basic bookshelf navigation model remains.

If that does not happen: If online services do not work, Wi-Fi may be off or Airplane Mode may be on.

Practice: Switch bookcases three times and identify each.

Part 6 - Basic Playback

Task Key / Control
Play / pause Play/Stop
Jump back 5 seconds Short press Rewind
Jump forward 5 seconds Short press Fast Forward
Progressively rewind Press and hold Rewind
Progressively fast-forward Press and hold Fast Forward

6.1 Read an Audio Book

What you will learn: Start, pause, and review audio.

Before you begin: Select an audio book.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press Play/Stop.

  2. Listen for at least 20 seconds.

  3. Press Play/Stop to pause.

  4. Short-press Rewind.

  5. Press Play/Stop.

  6. Short-press Fast Forward.

  7. Pause again.

What should happen: Playback starts/stops and short presses move about five seconds in audio content.

If that does not happen: In text content, short Rewind/Fast Forward can move by line and held presses use percentage jumps instead of audio time.

Practice: Practice until you can use all four playback controls by touch.

Part 7 - Navigation by Heading, Page, Time, and Text

7.1 Choose a Navigation Level

What you will learn: Move by the structure supported by the current book.

Before you begin: Open a structured DAISY or text book.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 2 or 8.

  2. Listen to the current navigation level.

  3. Continue pressing 2/8 until you hear the level you want, such as heading, page, time jump, paragraph, line, word, or character.

  4. Press 4 to move backward at that level.

  5. Press 6 to move forward.

What should happen: The Stream moves according to the selected level.

If that does not happen: Not every book contains every navigation level. If 'page' or a heading level is absent, the book may not include it.

Practice: Navigate by at least three different levels.

Text navigation: Text-to-speech books can expose paragraph, sentence, word, and character navigation. HTML/XML/DOCX/EPUB content may also expose headings.

Part 8 - Go To Commands

8.1 Go Directly to a Page or Heading

What you will learn: Jump instead of stepping repeatedly.

Before you begin: Use a book that contains the requested structure.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press the square Go To key above number 1.

  2. Listen to the type of destination requested.

  3. Enter the number with the numeric keypad.

  4. Press Confirm (#).

What should happen: The Stream moves to that page/heading/destination.

If that does not happen: If the number does not exist, the Stream announces an error or remains near the current position.

Practice: Go to two valid pages or headings.

  • Go To can also support time, percentage, beginning/end, book number, and online-service actions depending on context.

  • While on a bookshelf, Go To can be used to enter a relative book number, then Confirm.

Part 9 - Where Am I and Info

9.1 Ask Where Am I?

What you will learn: Hear current reading position without changing it.

Before you begin: A book is open.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 5.

  2. Listen to title and current position information.

  3. Press and hold 5 when you want additional device/book identification information such as serial/model information.

What should happen: The Stream reports context without moving your place.

If that does not happen: Information varies by book type.

Practice: Use Where Am I? in an audio book and a text book.

9.2 Hear General Information

What you will learn: Review battery, storage, book, and device information.

Before you begin: The Stream is on.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 0.

  2. Use 4 and 6 if multiple information items are available.

  3. Listen for battery, storage, title, position, or device information.

  4. Press Cancel (*) to leave if needed.

What should happen: You can review status without opening Settings.

If that does not happen: Exact items vary with current content and device state.

Practice: Find battery status and one storage/device item.

Part 10 - Sleep Timer and Clock

10.1 Set a Sleep Timer

What you will learn: Have the Stream stop after a chosen interval.

Before you begin: A book is playing or paused.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press Sleep once.

  2. Listen to the announced timer.

  3. Press Sleep repeatedly to cycle available timer values.

  4. Stop on the desired time.

  5. To cancel, cycle until Sleep off is announced.

What should happen: The player will stop/shut down according to the configured sleep behavior.

If that does not happen: Available values can be customized in Settings; common choices include 15, 30, 45, and 60 minutes.

Practice: Set 15 minutes and then turn Sleep off.

Time/date: Press and hold Sleep to hear the current time and date.

Part 11 - Bookmarks

11.1 Insert and Return to a Bookmark

What you will learn: Mark a location for later.

Before you begin: Open a book.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Move to the location.

  2. Press Bookmark.

  3. Follow the spoken prompt to insert a bookmark and confirm if requested.

  4. Continue reading elsewhere.

  5. Use the bookmark navigation options to return to the saved marker.

What should happen: The Stream stores the bookmark for the book.

If that does not happen: Bookmark behavior can vary by content type. Audio, highlight, and numbered bookmarks are also supported.

Practice: Create and return to two bookmarks.

Text-entry role: The diamond Bookmark key also changes lowercase/uppercase/numeric text-entry modes when typing in fields such as Wi-Fi or account credentials.

Part 12 - Managing Books and Files

12.1 Use Manage Books

What you will learn: Perform context-specific file actions.

Before you begin: Focus a disposable book/file.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 3.

  2. Listen to available management actions.

  3. Use 4/6 or the announced navigation keys to review them.

  4. Choose an action only after hearing the selected title again.

  5. Press Confirm to proceed or Cancel to leave.

What should happen: The available actions reflect the current bookshelf/content.

If that does not happen: Deleting a book removes that local copy. Re-download may require internet/service access.

Practice: Open Manage Books on a practice file and cancel.

Part 13 - SD Card and Computer File Transfer

13.1 Insert an SD Card

What you will learn: Add removable storage.

Before you begin: Power can be on or off, but handle the card gently.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Locate the silicone-covered SD slot on the top edge.

  2. Open the cover.

  3. Orient the card correctly and insert it until seated.

  4. Wait for Stream detection and spoken feedback.

What should happen: The Stream detects the SD card and its supported bookshelf folders/content.

If that does not happen: Never force the card. If it is not recognized, power-cycle and verify formatting/card health.

Practice: Insert and identify an SD card if available.

13.2 Transfer Files from Windows

What you will learn: Copy supported content using USB-C.

Before you begin: Have a Windows computer and USB cable.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Connect the Stream to the computer using the bottom-center USB-C port.

  2. Wait for Windows to detect it as a portable/MTP device.

  3. Open File Explorer.

  4. Open the Stream's internal memory or SD-card storage.

  5. Copy supported files into appropriate folders/bookshelves.

  6. Use Windows' safe-device practices and disconnect the cable.

  7. Let the Stream rescan content.

What should happen: Transferred books/files appear on the relevant bookshelf.

If that does not happen: If the Stream is not detected, try another USB port/cable and unlock/restart the device if needed.

Practice: Transfer one harmless MP3 or text file.

Supported formats: Current NLS/HumanWare references list formats including DAISY, BRF, DOCX, EPUB, HTML, MP3, FLAC, OGG, WAV, TXT, XML, and others.

Part 14 - Text, EPUB, BRF, Music, Podcasts, and Notes

  • Text bookshelf: electronic text read through the Stream's text-to-speech voice.

  • BRF/braille files: handled as text/braille content with the configured braille translation table where applicable.

  • EPUB/HTML/DOCX: may expose heading navigation when the source file contains structure.

  • Music bookshelf: audio files and folders; music speed remains normal by default and bass/treble options are available.

  • Saved Podcasts: downloaded podcast episodes stored for offline playback.

  • Notes: recordings and note files created/managed on the device.

Accessibility principle: A well-structured source file gives better heading/page navigation than an unstructured text conversion.

Part 15 - Wi-Fi and Airplane Mode

15.1 Turn Wireless On

What you will learn: Disable Airplane Mode so Wi-Fi/Bluetooth can operate.

Before you begin: Wireless use is permitted where you are.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press and hold the Online key.

  2. Listen for Airplane Mode status.

  3. If it announces Airplane Mode on, press/hold again or use Settings to turn Airplane Mode off.

  4. Open Settings with 7.

  5. Navigate to Wireless and then Wi-Fi.

  6. Confirm Wi-Fi is on.

What should happen: Wireless features become available.

If that does not happen: HumanWare documents Airplane Mode as on by default; when it is on, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are disabled.

Practice: Turn Airplane Mode on and off once.

15.2 Connect to Wi-Fi

What you will learn: Join a wireless network directly on the Stream.

Before you begin: Know the network name and password.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 7 for Settings.

  2. Navigate to Wireless > Wi-Fi > New Connection.

  3. Choose Scan for connection.

  4. Use 4/6 to find the network and press Confirm.

  5. Enter the Wi-Fi password with multi-tap text entry.

  6. Use Bookmark to switch lowercase, uppercase, and numeric entry when needed.

  7. Press Confirm.

  8. Check Wi-Fi Status afterward.

What should happen: The Stream connects and announces network status.

If that does not happen: If the password is rejected, check case, punctuation, and text-entry mode. Use Key Describer/User Guide to relearn multi-tap entry if needed.

Practice: Connect to a trusted Wi-Fi network.

Part 16 - Bluetooth

16.1 Pair Bluetooth Headphones or Speaker

What you will learn: Use wireless audio output.

Before you begin: Airplane Mode is off and the Bluetooth device is in pairing mode.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 7.

  2. Navigate to Wireless > Bluetooth.

  3. Turn Bluetooth on.

  4. Choose the option to scan/pair a device.

  5. Use 4/6 through discovered device names.

  6. Press Confirm on the intended device.

  7. Wait for connected confirmation.

What should happen: Stream audio routes to the paired Bluetooth device.

If that does not happen: If the device is not found, put it back into pairing mode and move it closer. Airplane Mode must be off.

Practice: Pair and disconnect a noncritical Bluetooth audio device.

Privacy: Bluetooth device names can reveal personal names/model information. Verify the exact device before pairing in public places.

Part 17 - NLS BARD

Eligible U.S. NLS patrons can configure BARD on the Stream 3 and search/download books over Wi-Fi.

17.1 Add an NLS BARD Account

What you will learn: Enable the BARD online bookshelf.

Before you begin: You have active BARD credentials and Wi-Fi is connected.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 7 for Settings.

  2. Use navigation to open Online settings.

  3. Open Book Services.

  4. Move to NLS BARD.

  5. Choose Add account and press Confirm.

  6. Enter the BARD email address with multi-tap entry, then Confirm.

  7. Enter the password, using Bookmark to switch case/numeric mode, then Confirm.

  8. Wait for the service-enabled announcement.

What should happen: An NLS BARD bookshelf appears in the online bookcase.

If that does not happen: If validation fails, verify the same credentials on the BARD website and check Wi-Fi. Do not repeatedly guess passwords if the account may lock.

Practice: Add the account only when you are ready to protect the credentials.

17.2 Search and Download a BARD Book

What you will learn: Find BARD content directly from the Stream.

Before you begin: BARD is configured and Wi-Fi works.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Tap Online to enter the online bookcase.

  2. Press 1 until NLS BARD is announced.

  3. Use Go To to reach Search or use 4/6 to the Search option.

  4. Press Confirm.

  5. Use 2/8 through search types and Confirm the desired one.

  6. Enter the search text.

  7. Review results with 4/6.

  8. Press 5 for a short description.

  9. Press Bookmark to add a result to the BARD wish list when available.

  10. Press Confirm to download, or Play/Stop to begin reading the selected result when supported.

What should happen: The book downloads or begins reading according to the selected action.

If that does not happen: If no results appear, broaden the search. If downloading fails, check Wi-Fi and BARD status.

Practice: Search for one known author or title.

NLS authorization: NLS books also require the Stream to be authorized. HumanWare provides online authorization/key-download procedures; see Part 21.

Part 18 - Bookshare, NFB-NEWSLINE, Internet Radio, Podcasts, and TuneIn

  • Bookshare: configured under Online settings > Book Services using eligible account credentials.

  • NFB-NEWSLINE: available to eligible U.S. users when configured.

  • Internet Radio: search and save stations using online services.

  • Podcasts: search/add feeds and download/manage episodes.

  • TuneIn: can be used without an account for basic listening; account pairing enables library/favorites features.

  • Wikipedia/Wiktionary references: available as online reference features in supported configurations.

Availability: Online services can change independently of the Stream firmware. If a service disappears, verify current HumanWare support information before resetting the device.

Part 19 - Recording and Audio Notes

19.1 Make a Recording

What you will learn: Create a spoken note with the built-in microphone.

Before you begin: Use a quiet environment and respect recording consent.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Locate the Record button near the top of the right side.

  2. Press Record to start according to the current recording configuration.

  3. Speak toward the small built-in microphone hole above Go To.

  4. Use the Record control again to stop/save as prompted.

  5. Find the recording in the Notes/recording location.

What should happen: A recording is created in the configured format/location.

If that does not happen: If the recording is silent, check recording source and volume settings.

Practice: Record a ten-second non-sensitive note.

External recording: The bottom-left-side microphone/line-in jack can accept an external source. Settings control preferred source, recording volume, file type, and save location.

Part 20 - Settings and Software Updates

20.1 Navigate Settings

What you will learn: Change one setting at a time and verify the result.

Before you begin: The Stream is on.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 7.

  2. Use the announced navigation keys to move among major categories such as General, Navigation/Playback, Wireless, Recording, and Online settings.

  3. Press Confirm to enter a category.

  4. Use 4/6 or 2/8 as announced to review choices.

  5. Press Confirm to accept or Cancel to back out.

What should happen: The chosen setting changes only after confirmation.

If that does not happen: If you become lost, press Cancel repeatedly and reopen Settings.

Practice: Find Language, Sleep configuration, and Wireless without changing them.

20.2 Check for Updates

What you will learn: Install current Stream software when appropriate.

Before you begin: Connect to stable Wi-Fi and preferably AC power.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 7.

  2. Navigate to Online settings > Software Update.

  3. Choose Check for updates.

  4. Press Confirm.

  5. Follow spoken prompts if an update is available.

  6. Do not interrupt power during installation.

What should happen: The Stream reports current software or installs an available update.

If that does not happen: If the update fails, restore stable Wi-Fi/power and retry. HumanWare also provides manual SD-card update packages.

Practice: Check for updates without forcing an unnecessary reinstall.

Part 21 - NLS Authorization

21.1 Refresh NLS User Keys over Wi-Fi

What you will learn: Authorize NLS playback when HumanWare/NLS instructs you to do so.

Before you begin: Wi-Fi is connected.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press 7 until General Settings is reached.

  2. Navigate to Online Settings and press Confirm.

  3. Open Software Update.

  4. Move to Check for updates.

  5. Press Confirm.

  6. Wait for the server check and listen for a user-keys processed message when applicable.

What should happen: The Stream receives available authorization keys.

If that does not happen: If NLS books remain unauthorized, follow HumanWare's current NLS authorization support steps or contact NLS/HumanWare rather than repeatedly changing unrelated settings.

Practice: Know where this command is, even if authorization is already working.

Part 22 - Troubleshooting

22.1 No Sound

What you will learn: Check playback and output before resetting.

Before you begin: A book is selected.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press Play/Stop.

  2. Short-press Power until Volume is selected.

  3. Press the left-side Up control several times.

  4. Unplug headphones if you expect the built-in speaker.

  5. Disconnect Bluetooth if audio may be routed wirelessly.

What should happen: Audio becomes audible through the intended output.

If that does not happen: If not, try another book and headphones, then inspect settings.

Practice: Practice identifying the current audio output.

22.2 Cannot Find a Book

What you will learn: Rebuild your location: bookcase, bookshelf, book.

Before you begin: Content should be present.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Tap Online to verify whether you are in offline or online bookcase.

  2. Press 1 repeatedly through bookshelves.

  3. Use 4/6 through titles.

  4. Use Go To book-number navigation if you know the relative position.

  5. Check the SD card/computer transfer if local content is missing.

What should happen: You locate the content or identify which storage/service is missing.

If that does not happen: Do not reset the Stream just because you are on the wrong bookshelf.

Practice: Find one local and one online title.

22.3 Wi-Fi Will Not Connect

What you will learn: Check Airplane Mode and credentials.

Before you begin: A known network is available.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press and hold Online and verify Airplane Mode is off.

  2. Open Wireless > Wi-Fi > Status.

  3. Rescan the network.

  4. Re-enter the password carefully, checking uppercase/lowercase/numeric mode with Bookmark.

  5. Move closer to the router.

What should happen: The Stream connects or gives a specific failure.

If that does not happen: If the network uses a captive portal or unsupported enterprise setup, direct Stream connection may not be possible.

Practice: Reconnect to a trusted network.

22.4 BARD Search or Download Fails

What you will learn: Separate service/account issues from basic Wi-Fi.

Before you begin: BARD is configured.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Verify Wi-Fi Status.

  2. Confirm another online service works if available.

  3. Open NLS BARD bookshelf.

  4. Retry a simple title/author search.

  5. If account validation fails, test the credentials on BARD outside the Stream.

  6. Check current HumanWare/NLS service notices before deleting the account.

What should happen: You identify whether the problem is connectivity, credentials, service availability, or content.

If that does not happen: Avoid removing a working account until simpler checks are completed.

Practice: Practice the diagnostic sequence.

22.5 Device Seems Frozen

What you will learn: Use safe recovery before reset.

Before you begin: The Stream does not respond normally.

Exactly how to do it

  1. Press Play/Stop and Cancel to see whether playback/menu focus is simply paused.

  2. Connect AC power.

  3. Press and hold Power for the documented shutdown/reset behavior.

  4. Restart and check Info for battery/software state.

What should happen: The Stream restarts or returns to normal operation.

If that does not happen: If it remains unresponsive, use HumanWare's current reset/troubleshooting guidance or support rather than forcing ports/cards.

Practice: Know the safe recovery order.
