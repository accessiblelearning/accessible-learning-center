Excel Lesson 7

Managing Worksheets and Cross-Sheet References

Excel manual All Excel lessons My progress

Goal

Organize related information across worksheets and create a summary that refers to another sheet.

Skills practiced

  • Insert a worksheet with Shift+F11.
  • Move between sheets with Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down.
  • Rename worksheets.
  • Reference a cell on another worksheet.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Create a blank workbook and rename Sheet1 to January. Use Alt+Q and search for Rename Sheet if ribbon KeyTips differ.
  2. Enter Category and Amount in A1 and B1.
  3. Enter five fictional January expenses in rows 2 through 6.
  4. Press Shift+F11 to insert a new worksheet and rename it February.
  5. Enter the same headers and five fictional February expenses.
  6. Press Shift+F11 again and rename the new worksheet Summary.
  7. Use Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down to move among the three worksheets.
  8. On January, calculate the total in B8 with =SUM(B2:B6). Do the same on February.
  9. On Summary, enter January Total in A1 and type =January!B8 in B1.
  10. Enter February Total in A2 and type =February!B8 in B2.
  11. Enter Combined Total in A3 and type =SUM(B1:B2) in B3.
  12. Save the workbook as Excel-Lesson-7.xlsx.

JAWS help: Press Insert+Spacebar, then J, to search JAWS commands when you forget a keystroke. Ribbon KeyTips can differ slightly by Microsoft 365 update, so Alt+Q search is included where useful.

Your assignment

Create January, February, and Summary worksheets. Enter expenses on the monthly sheets and show both monthly totals plus a combined total on Summary.

Save the finished assignment as Excel-Lesson-7.xlsx.

Before submitting

  • All three worksheets have meaningful names.
  • Monthly totals use SUM formulas.
  • Summary references the monthly worksheets.
  • Ctrl+Page Up and Ctrl+Page Down move among the sheets.
  • The file is saved as Excel-Lesson-7.xlsx.
