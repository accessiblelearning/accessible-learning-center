AI Fundamentals Lesson 7

Summarize and Organize Information Responsibly

AI Fundamentals manualAll AI Fundamentals lessonsMy progress

Goal

Turn public information into a structured summary, then compare the summary with the source.

Before you begin

  • Use only fictional or public information.
  • Do not enter client, medical, financial, password, confidential workplace, or personally identifying information.
  • Do not purchase a subscription or start a paid trial.
  • You—not the AI tool—are responsible for the final work.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open the AI Fundamentals and Responsible Use manual and review the prompting, verification, privacy, and screen-reader sections.
  2. Open an available AI tool in a regular browser tab. If an account or feature is unavailable, study the sample prompt and write the expected process instead.
  3. Navigate by headings and form controls. Find the prompt edit field and confirm its label before typing.
  4. Use this practice prompt or adapt it without adding private information:
    Act as an accessible workplace skills coach. Help me turn public information into a structured summary, then compare the summary with the source. Use fictional or public information only. Organize the response with descriptive headings and numbered steps. Identify anything I must verify.
  5. Read the complete response by heading, paragraph, list item, and link. Do not assume the first answer is correct.
  6. Send at least one follow-up prompt that improves clarity, accuracy, accessibility, or completeness.
  7. Verify important claims using the original source or another authoritative source. Record unsupported statements and corrections.
  8. Edit the final result in your own words. Remove generic, inaccurate, biased, or unnecessary content.
  9. Create a short process note naming the tool, prompts used, facts checked, changes made, and remaining uncertainty.
  10. Save the assignment as ai-fundamentals-Lesson-7.txt.

Screen-reader help: JAWS and NVDA users can press H for headings, B for buttons, and E for edit fields in browse mode. Narrator users can navigate with Scan Mode. Press Tab and Shift+Tab for interactive controls. Because AI interfaces change frequently, listen to each control’s current accessible name.

Your assignment

Turn public information into a structured summary, then compare the summary with the source.

Include the original prompt, at least one follow-up prompt, a revised result, a verification record, and a one-paragraph reflection on what required human judgment.

Save it as ai-fundamentals-Lesson-7.txt. DOCX, PDF, and BRF versions are also accepted.

Before submitting

  • No private or confidential information is included.
  • The result uses descriptive headings and clear organization.
  • Important claims were checked against an authoritative source.
  • The student—not the AI tool—made the final decisions.
  • The filename is ai-fundamentals-Lesson-7.txt.
