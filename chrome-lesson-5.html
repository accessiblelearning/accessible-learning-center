Chrome Lesson 5

Bookmarks, History, and Finding a Page Again

Chrome manualAll Chrome lessonsMy progress

Goal

Save useful pages and recover previously visited information.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open a safe page that will be useful later.
  2. Press Ctrl+D to open the bookmark dialog.
  3. Give the bookmark a clear name and save it in an appropriate folder.
  4. Press Ctrl+Shift+B to show or hide the bookmarks bar and observe how focus changes.
  5. Press Ctrl+Shift+O to open Bookmark Manager.
  6. Use Ctrl+F within Bookmark Manager to find the saved bookmark.
  7. Press Ctrl+H to open History.
  8. Search History for the practice page and open the correct result.
  9. Remove only the practice bookmark if instructed; do not clear shared browsing history.
  10. Write the difference between bookmarks and history in Chrome-Lesson-5.txt.

Screen-reader help: With JAWS, press Insert+T for the window title, Insert+Tab for focused-control information, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Chrome controls and labels can change after browser updates.
