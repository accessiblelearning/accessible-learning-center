Read, Pan, Route the Cursor, and Select Text

Focus 40 Blue manualAll Focus lessonsMy progress

Goal

Submit a command chart for panning, navigation, cursor routing, and text selection.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open a short instructor-provided document with JAWS.
  2. Use the left and right panning buttons to move through the braille line.
  3. Use rocker bars or configured NAV rockers to move by line, sentence, or paragraph.
  4. Press a cursor router above a braille cell to move the editing cursor to that location.
  5. Practice selecting a short passage with the documented Shift and cursor-router method.
  6. Cancel the selection or undo any accidental edit, then save only a practice copy.

JAWS and braille tip: Use Insert+T to confirm the window title, Insert+Tab to identify focus, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Focus commands vary by device generation, JAWS settings, application, and connection method; use the official guide linked from the manual when a command differs.
