Excel Lesson 2

Navigating, Editing, Finding, and Filling Data

Excel manual All Excel lessons My progress

Goal

Move efficiently through a worksheet, edit cells, find and replace information, and copy values without relying on a mouse.

Skills practiced

  • Move to data boundaries with Ctrl+Arrow.
  • Use Ctrl+G to go directly to a cell.
  • Edit a cell with F2.
  • Use Find and Replace.
  • Copy and paste a value or formula.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open Excel-Lesson-1.xlsx.
  2. Press Ctrl+Home to move to A1. Press Ctrl+End to move to the last used cell.
  3. Press Ctrl+G, type B3, and press Enter to move directly to cell B3.
  4. Press F2, change the department in B3 to Customer Service, and press Enter.
  5. Press Ctrl+F, type Employment, and press Enter to locate that word. Press Escape to close Find.
  6. Press Ctrl+H. Replace Training with Learning Services, then close the dialog.
  7. Add a fourth column header in D1 named Status.
  8. Type Active in D2. Copy D2 with Ctrl+C, select D3 through the last employee row with Shift+Down Arrow, and press Ctrl+V.
  9. Review the list with Ctrl+Arrow and Arrow keys.
  10. Press F12 or use File, Save As, and save a copy as Excel-Lesson-2.xlsx.

JAWS help: Press Insert+Spacebar, then J, to search JAWS commands when you forget a keystroke. Ribbon KeyTips can differ slightly by Microsoft 365 update, so Alt+Q search is included where useful.

Your assignment

Update the contact list so one department is Customer Service, Training is replaced with Learning Services, and every employee has Active in the Status column.

Save the finished assignment as Excel-Lesson-2.xlsx.

Before submitting

  • Customer Service and Learning Services appear in the Department column.
  • Every employee row has Active in the Status column.
  • The file is saved as Excel-Lesson-2.xlsx.
