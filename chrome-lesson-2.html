Chrome Lesson 2

Tabs, Windows, and Restoring Work

Chrome manualAll Chrome lessonsMy progress

Goal

Open, switch, close, reopen, and organize browser tabs and windows.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Press Ctrl+T to open a new tab.
  2. Open a safe practice website from the address bar.
  3. Press Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab to move forward and backward among tabs.
  4. Press Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+8 to move to a numbered tab and Ctrl+9 to move to the last tab.
  5. Press Ctrl+W to close only the active tab.
  6. Press Ctrl+Shift+T to reopen the most recently closed tab.
  7. Press Ctrl+N to open a new Chrome window.
  8. Use Alt+Tab to move between Chrome windows and other applications.
  9. Close the extra Chrome window with Alt+F4 after confirming it is the intended window.
  10. Write a tab-and-window guide in Chrome-Lesson-2.txt.

Screen-reader help: With JAWS, press Insert+T for the window title, Insert+Tab for focused-control information, and Insert+1 for Keyboard Help. Chrome controls and labels can change after browser updates.
